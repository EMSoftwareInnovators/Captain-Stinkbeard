# Story Phase 15: The Great Mellowing

*Captain Stinkbeard and the One Fart He Actually Misses.* The fourteenth canonical story
chapter. Internally: THE GREAT MELLOWING ARC (or BROGATH'S ECONOMIC RECOVERY). It picks up the
morning after Mandatory History Night (the end of Phase 14), still inside the Great
Sharkstorm, with Brogath the Bashful living aboard the Queen Anne's Revenge for good, and ends
a few days later with the captain at the rail, throwing a pillow at a re-forming spectral
customer and saying "Not today."

It adapts eleven source arcs as one escalating story, and the causal chain is the point:
**the TV weaponises Brogath's history; his embarrassment turns green; the fake economy
becomes real cargo; the cargo brings visitors; the visitors bring the beard disaster; the
beard disaster brings the Grand Nap; a careless word brings a bank; a chicken brings the
Great Mellowing; the haze shuts the chaos down; the bank closes because Brogath stops being
bashful; and the captain ends up missing one specific Brogath fart.**

Five movements:

1. **The Stenchmaster Channel refuses to die** (chapters 170 to 172). The Mark II has been on
   for three days, unplugged. The crew have a **Brogath Television Safety Protocol** (Pete on
   blush watch, Gristle with the blanket, Bob on loose paper, Jim on fabric, Squawks forbidden
   to flap, Garrick wanting snacks: NO BEANS). **The Age of Fart Commerce** (actually rich;
   Ledger Billiam's two ledgers; No-Nonsense Tilda's hat, which was GREEN; the bakery, the
   cobbler, the rope merchant's bucket, the farmer's shed, the forge, the boat going
   backwards, the Grand Gust Exchange, the funeral), with five commercial breaks that
   escalate (one sash, two, slow motion, unsupported, mass synchronized flutter), each needing
   a different piece of nonsense to steady him. Straight on: **The Top Ten Most Embarrassing
   Sash Flutters**; number one is the goat; Garrick laughs; the **first skunk-weed waft**
   (green). The captain breathes it through his beard and sees Ancient Grand Stenchmasters
   (a hallucination layer); on deck it clears; he tears the Mark II's valve, wire and
   horseshoe out (`tv_destroyed_again`, for now). Brogath goes quiet: **his crew**. The
   cardboard plan to bring them all back (stopped: the box, Garrick's sketch of Elsie, a kind
   word), the pocket edition's Appendix Nine in the zone, "They were family," and Elsie
   Beanwright, never.
2. **Brogath wants to be useful** (173 to 174). He surveys everything he's broken, sees the
   20,000 Grand Tokens and has A GRAND STENCHMASTER'S GRAND IDEA. The captain counts his
   **emergency reserve** (seven real doubloons under the cabin floorboard), the crate goes up
   on deck, everybody stands clear, and Garrick runs a "controlled productivity exercise"
   (mocks Ledger Billiam, the sash, atmospheric economics; steps on a token; insults the crew).
   WRATH, and the **Economic Anger Waft**: tokens and reserve become a mountain of bargain-bin
   **Skunk Weed** (fictional), once. Creative conversion, sorting (stems, seeds, leaf, dust,
   one ESPECIALLY SKUNKY crate), the Economic Recovery Report, and the Bling Bling King:
   POTENTIAL CUSTOMER ACQUISITION; ACCEPTS GOLD, DOES NOT ACCEPT FARTS.
3. **The visitors** (175 to 177). The Sharkstorm spits a little modern boat (SECOND THOUGHTS)
   onto the deck, with two very relaxed grown men in it (Stoner #1 and Stoner #2). An awkward
   sale (STINKBEARD'S SELECT SCENT OF SKUNK, branding unapproved; Grand Tokens refused;
   forty modern dollars accepted on Pete's advice), a **safe corner** at the bow, a tour, and
   what a Grand Stenchmaster fart is ("a natural disaster with an embarrassing backstory").
   The Mark II switches itself back on with no insides (`tv_possessed_reactivation`; the crew
   are angry, not surprised): the Wrath of Brogath, and **Brogath and the Chicken** (Agnes).
   The clambake idea, and the rule: **Stenchmaster farts are not selectable on demand.** In the
   zone, the cursed **beard-smoke** experiment (off-screen, four seconds), and the **Grand
   Paranoia**: Grand Stenchmaster Threat Levels 1 to 6 (LEVEL 6: EVACUATE REALITY), guard
   training, Mop Brogath, and the captain's rules (NO BEARD JOINTS, and no beard merchandise;
   five stars from the King, who won't stock it).
4. **The Grand Nap and the Bashfulness Branch** (178 to 180). Garrick decrees THE GRAND
   STENCHMASTER'S GRAND NAP (it's a nap). The old bedding is stripped; clean Cheap-O-Rama
   sashes become pads, blankets and pillows ("fart-themed is better than fart-scented");
   Garrick reads the **sash-backed loan** in the pocket edition; a growling old pillow goes
   into the storm (SHARKSTORM DOES NOT ACCEPT PREDATORY PILLOWS). The captain wakes with a nose
   full of beard, thinks he's in the zone, and speed-runs the Brogath Rules aloud with Brogath
   in the room: the **Nose-Fire Waft** (tiny nostril flames all round), a pillow that learns to
   roar (caught three times, thrown out), an apology, and Brogath's concentrated embarrassment
   builds **THE BASHFULNESS BRANCH**: OPEN SO LONG AS BROGATH REMAINS BASHFUL (a compliment
   makes it flicker; he blushes at the compliment and it's back). The night depositors (Duke
   Gasimir: ALL OF IT; Countess Flatulencia, compounded; Baron Billowmere; the Dowager
   Puffenstein), served by the branch's own teller while the captain tries to sleep. DING.
5. **The Great Mellowing, and after** (181 to 185). A chicken on a parachute: **Agnes the
   Second**. She sits on Brogath's foot and pecks his sash; it flutters; he goes pink; and the
   green one comes out, through the whole ship and out into the Sharkstorm: **the Great
   Mellowing**. Everybody acts differently; the captain is the only one sober (his beard is too
   full to let it in). The Mark II shows a sash advert and nobody is at their station (the
   captain does every job; Pete can't remember "licensed"); the **Untold Stories**; the
   branch's teller clocks out and the captain is **RELIEF GRAND TELLER** (Teller Shift V2):
   Boomington, Whistlewaft, Fumblebottom, the Wind Brothers, Grandmother Boom-Boom,
   Rumblequill, Lady Flapworthy, **Gary** (lunch), Admiral Thunderpants, and Emperor
   Farthelmew (who realises mid-deposit that none of it is real). 2.3 / 5. CUSTOMERS FOUND
   TELLER HOSTILE. The AFTER-HOURS EXPRESS WINDOW: Grand Lady Whifflebottom's 47 silver
   (forms 1A to 97Q; a goat in a cathedral; a spoon in the goat), the collateral activation
   ceremony: the sash destroyed, the loan cancelled, the fees nonrefundable. Brogath is too
   mellow to be bashful and the branch closes for good. Peace. Munchies. **Vindicta Thunderwaft
   the Unserved** won't dissolve, and her blast clears the haze; normality returns (decrees,
   committees, flutter panics, sharks biting) and it's terrible. **Agnes the Second is gone**,
   into the storm, on a pillow. The captain asks Brogath to do it again; he can't (a
   controlled flutter, the goat, the wedding cake: ordinary stink each time). The **Emergency
   Mellowing Supply** goes into the captain's rules. Not today.

The phase starts from the end of Phase 14: Pete, in the galley (talk to him).

About two and a half to three hours of play:

- 16 chapters (170 to 185) and 16 quests with 67 objectives.
- 174 story flags.
- About 1,350 lines of dialogue and narration in the scripts, about 165 lines of crew chatter
  across the stages (every crew member has Mellowing lines), and 15 bank customers with their
  own lines.
- No new map. The deck, galley, quarters, cabin, zone and hold change through map patches.
- No battles. The challenges are Brogath Stability (steadying him through the commercials,
  his crew, the report, the mellow advert), the timing bars (prying the Mark II apart, the
  pillows, knots, braces), the threat-level choices, the teller's window (V2) and the loan
  forms.

## Continuity

established, the Grand Bank concluded business, the Mark II is possessed (`ses_mk2_state` =
`possessed`), the storm is in `rough_turbulence`, and the captain holds 20,000 Grand Tokens

**The captain has said Brogath is part of the crew** (Phase 14); this phase repeats and means

**Nothing after the source.** The phase ends with "Not today." Agnes is not rescued, nobody
from Brogath's crew comes back, the Sharkstorm is not resolved, Garrick stays Grand
Stenchmaster, Brogath is not solved, nobody makes a Steel Brogath. The visitors are still
aboard (the batch doesn't say they leave). `tests/phase15_story.test.js` checks that no
Phase 15 script sets any of those flags or spawns any ancient crew member.

## Systems added (reusable)

**Ship Atmosphere State** (`src/systems/atmosphere.js`, the `atmosphere` command). One saved
value; each state is authored consequences, not a simulation: a whole-room haze tint
(`haze: { level, color }`, used where a room has no haze of its own), `npcPace` (wanderers and
routines move slower in the Mellowing), and optional `music`/`ambience` on the ship's maps.
Dialogue, placements and looks read it with ordinary `value` conditions. Off the ship's maps it
is NORMAL.

**Brogath MELLOW** (`src/systems/stability.js`). A subject may have
`"mellow": { "value", "in", "triggerScale" }`; while the value is in the list, positive
pressure counts for `triggerScale` and the state reads MELLOW (ANGRY still wins). Clearing
the atmosphere restores everything at once. Running incidents climb at the same scale.

**Channel episodes** (the `channelEpisode` command). An episode lists `segments`: beat
ranges, reaction scripts, and commercials (`{ episode, program?, trigger, reassure }`). The
command plays them in order (and sets the TV's state and the episode's `doneFlag`). New
episodes and commercials are data only.

**One-time conversions** (`data/story/conversions.json`, the `convert` command). Variables are
recorded and emptied, items taken, cargo added, behind one flag; running it again does
nothing. The data never names the purse (the validator refuses `gold`).

**Teller Shift V2** (`src/systems/bank.js` `TELLER_MODIFIERS`): `forgets_title`,
`form_backwards`, `sash_wrong`, `whistle`, `shared`, `long_sash`, `mellow_assistants`,
`desk_slides`, `tv_interrupt`, `brogath_risk`. Each reshapes the same beats (no new
controls). The `bank` command's `observe: true` has a bank's own teller serve a queue (the
night depositors: their `observed` scripts play instead of the window and the ledger; the
queue still moves).

**Hallucinations** reuse Phase 13's alias sets (`data/story/aliases/phase15.json`:
`skunk_haze_vision`, `beard_paranoia`): names, portraits, looks and a tint while a flag holds;
ids, quests and saves stay the crew's; nothing can be fought.

**The chicken painter** (`src/art/characters/chickenPainter.js`): field sheet and portrait.

## Prerequisites

Phase 15 needs the end of Phase 14 (`p14_complete`): Brogath permanent, the zone, the 20,000
tokens, the possessed Mark II. It starts when the captain talks to Pete in the galley
(`p15_started`); elsewhere a one-time hint says so. Every prerequisite listed by the brief
exists in the repository; none is faked.

state is ordinary story state (flags, vars, values), so old saves load unchanged and simply
read as NORMAL atmosphere, branch inactive, no cargo.
