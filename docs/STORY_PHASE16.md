# Story Phase 16: The Grand Lighter

*Captain Stinkbeard and the Grand Lighter.* Internally: THE GRAND LIGHTER, STENCHMASTER FART
ZONE, HORNET CATASTROPHE AND GRAND IGNITION CRISIS. Twenty chapters (186 to 205), twenty quests.
It picks up a day after Phase 15's "Not today." (Vindicta has blown the Great Mellowing out of
the ship; Agnes the Second is in the Sharkstorm, going round on a pillow; the Bashfulness Branch
is closed for good; the visitors are still aboard; the Skunk Weed is cargo) and ends at the
counter in the old quarters, with a lighter that works sometimes, the captain shouting
**COMMON SENSE IS WINNING!**, and nothing after it. There is no chapter 206.

The causal chain: **everybody is themselves again, so the captain wants the ship mellow; nobody
has a light; the light comes out of the Sharkstorm and brings Vindicta back; Brogath tries for
the green one on purpose and gets Coffin Sock; so the ship needs a room for releases; Vindicta
needs it at once, with Agnes inside; Agnes back means Chicken-Town, so the Great Mellowing
returns; mellow Brogath makes a dispensary; the dispensary's takings make a bank again; the bank
makes credit and a converter; Brogath wants to be useful and makes hornets; the hornets want the
captain's beard and the bank's credit; the bunker gets the lore and the portable set; the
documentary brings the Bureau back; the Bureau needs a hearing and Vindicta to light a cigarette
with the Crew Quarters; and the lighter wins.**

## Systems (new and reused)

- **Rooms with their own air** (extends Phase 15's ship atmosphere): `room_atmosphere_<map>`
  story values; `{ "atmosphere": "COFFIN_SOCK", "room": "galley" }`, `"SHIP"` hands one back,
  `"clearRooms": true` hands them all back. New states COFFIN_SOCK, VINDICTA_RELEASE,
  HORNET_SWARM_RESIDUE. Still no gas simulation.
- **The Stenchmaster Fart Zone** (`data/story/fart_zone.json`, `src/systems/fartZone.js`, the
  `fartZone` command): IDLE / QUEUED / ENTERING / PREPARING / RELEASING / VENTILATING / COMPLETE,
  one occupant, the queue, the door and portholes, damage, and each authored release processed
  exactly once (`fz_done_<id>`); a release against procedure is refused. Its own map
  (`stenchmaster_fart_zone`), reached by the warp on the cargo grating once it's open. Separate
  from the **Fart-Free Zone**, which becomes the crew's bunker (and stays theirs).
- **The captain's beard** (`data/story/beard.json`, `src/systems/beard.js`, the `beard`
  command, the BEARD PROFILE logbook): GARRICK_ORIGINAL, BROGATH_ANCIENT, GRAND_BANK_DEPOSITS,
  SKUNK_HAZE, COFFIN_SOCK, VINDICTA_LEGENDARY, GRAND_LIGHTER_FUEL, CURRENCY_CONVERTER
  (permanent) and HORNET_ATTRACTED (the only one that can be removed).
- **The Grand Lighter** (`data/story/novelty.json`, `src/systems/novelty.js`, the `lighter`
  command): a malfunction profile with a fixed sequence (click, nothing, spark, a brown puff, a
  smell, a sputter, a flame) and a saved counter; a scene that needs a flame forces the last
  attempt. Unreliable, never random. Obviously fictional.
- **Proceedings** (`data/story/proceedings/`, `src/systems/proceedings.js`, the `proceeding`
  command): a **form** (the 97 pages as curated questions and page-number montages; a required
  question re-asked on an invalid answer, at most three times), a **hearing** (points to a
  pass mark; one retry; then the Council passes it out of exhaustion), a **pitch** (arguments
  once each, a pressure gauge climbing a notch a round on the vista, a checkpoint with a longer
  clock: always winnable). Played with the ordinary dialogue choices.
- **The counter** (`data/story/bank/grand_counter.json`): one service counter, several skins
  (`counter_department`: DISPENSARY, BANK, BUREAU). It extends the Phase 14 bank: same scale,
  window and ledger; new customer `payment` kinds (`tokens` into the till, `check`,
  `application` for credit) and an `appraise` step.
- **Grand Sashback and the converter** (`data/story/credit.json`, `src/systems/credit.js`, the
  `appraise` and `currencyConverter` commands): the score comes from the notes the captain
  smells (top, heart, base), clamped 300-999; welcome bonuses are story tokens; the converter
  takes tokens out of a story variable and gives back only Coffin Sock (and a receipt). Neither
  ever touches the purse; the policy caps conversions at 1,000; nothing doubles.
- **The hornet swarm** (`data/hazards/hornets.json`, `src/systems/hazards/hornets.js`,
  `src/world/HornetSwarm.js`, the `hornets` command): DORMANT, SCATTERED, ALERT, SWARMING,
  BEARD_ATTRACTED, BANK_INVADING, BUNKER_SIEGE, DISPERSING. A pooled handful of small sprites
  per room (at most 48), some orbiting the captain's beard, the rest gathering at an anchor;
  they never block the way, never sting, and fade out when they disperse.
- **Brogath RECOVERING** (a fourth state, after MELLOW): after a big release his rises count
  half (`recoverFlag`, `recoverScale`) until a script lets him out (`{ "stability": "recover",
  "on": false }`). New triggers (Agnes clucking and flapping, being blamed, "Is that all?"),
  calms (the chicken just chickening, the procedure, a good swarm, being useful) and prompt
  sets (the near-miss, the swarm).
- **Two televisions**: the Mark II (galley) and the portable set (`ses_portable`, the
  Fart-Free Zone), each with its own power and state; the portable only gets the Stenchmaster
  Channel. **Vistas**: the bunker watching the portable set, the Grand Ignition Council with
  Vindicta's gauge; the galley legend vista for the lore book; the galley's Mark II for
  Cheapy's advert.
- **Conditional arrival points**: a spawn object can have an `if` (the grating's landing spot
  only counts once the grating is open).
- **Stingbeard** is a name variant on the captain behind `nickname_stingbeard`.

## Content rules kept

Skunk Weed stays fictional cargo; the "Emergency Mellowing Supply" is a smudge bowl under a
canopy, never prepared or described. The lighter is a joke, not a device; nothing explains how
to make a flame, wire anything or build anything. Nobody grows, prepares, doses or sources
anything. No animal is harmed (Agnes is rescued, the hornets never sting, the sharks are
fine). No filler combat: the phase starts no battle. The names are the game's own: GARRICK
GRUMBLEGUT, the Great Sharkstorm, the Queen Anne's Revenge. The tests scan the phase's data for
all of this (`tests/phase16_story.test.js`, "stays inside its brief").
