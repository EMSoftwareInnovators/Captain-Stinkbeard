# Story Phase 17: The Return of the Ancient Crew and the Coffin Sock Catastrophe

*Captain Stinkbeard and the Return of the Ancient Crew.* Seventeen chapters (206 to 222),
seventeen quests (and an eighteenth, the Great Ledger Expedition, left open on purpose). It picks
up after Phase 16's "COMMON SENSE IS WINNING" (the counter is the Bureau again; Agnes the Second
is back and a bit bald; the ship is mellow; the Sharkstorm goes round) and ends in the old
quarters after the Coffin Sock catastrophe: the ship partly recovered, Brogath proud of himself,
everyone furious, and nothing after it. There is no chapter 223.

The causal chain: **Brogath remembers his old crew in the Fart-Free Zone; he paints them as
cut-outs; he tries the treasure-room door to give them "a little help"; a plume of the old cloud
gets out through the crack, splits in two and goes into Ledger's and Tilda's cut-outs; they're
back, as cardboard, and join the crew; the geniuses take charge; Tilda wants a light and files
for one; the documentary tells them what became of them; Ledger remembers his books (Greyhook
Cove: blocked, the ship is inside the storm) and Tilda steers for them; Ledger finds a token and
invents Captain Minutes; the King laughs at all of it; Tilda's permit means a Fire-Waft; the
Grand Restraint fails when a shark hits the hull; the Fire-Waft sets the Sharkstorm alight and
singes Tilda; Agnes puts it out; Lord Thunderbottom comes to the Bureau, threatens Agnes and
releases with her in the room; the captain gets her out and his beard takes the blast; the beard
starts to puff; it stirs the Coffin Sock in Brogath; Garrick's Beard Joint and Frog Grog give the
captain the Grand Paranoia Spiral; he calls Jim Princess Stenchalina; Brogath lets go.**

## Systems (new and reused)

Reused, unchanged in kind: the party, battle engine and status page; the Fart Zone and its
posted procedure (queue, enter, door, portholes, release once, ventilate, complete); the
Fart-Free Zone; rooms' own air; the beard's contamination profile; the proceedings (the same
97-page form engine as Phase 16); the counter's Bureau skin; the converter; the Mark II and the
TV programme/segment system; vistas; inserts; aliases (Phase 11-13); Brogath's stability meter;
placement tables; debug presets.

New in Phase 17 (engine commit f2a2665 and after):

- **Party members who are also NPCs**: `joinParty` with `"level": "leader"` joins at the
  captain's level (full heal), never twice; `learnAbility` adds a story-taught ability once.
  A character with `"npc": "<id>"` speaks as that NPC (one speaker, not two). Saves drop a
  duplicated member (migration 16 → 17 and `Party.load`).
- **Traits** (`data/story/traits.json`): CARDBOARD_REINCARNATION, FLAT_BODY (evasion),
  FUME_ANIMATED (immune to sickness), LIGHTWEIGHT, FIRE_SUSCEPTIBLE (fire hurts more). Read by
  `Combatant` and the battle engine; shown on the status page.
- **Ledger and Tilda in battle**: their own resources (Accounts, ACC; Authority, AUTH) and
  abilities (CREATIVE ACCOUNTING, RECONCILE!, PROJECTED PROFITS, QUESTIONABLE VALUATION, CAPTAIN
  MINUTES; BROGATH, STOP!, NO NONSENSE!, HOLD THE LINE, AUTHORITATIVE REBUKE, FIRST MATE'S
  ORDERS), seven statuses (one, STAGGERED, makes a foe lose its turn). Battle sprites follow the
  field appearance (Tilda singed stays singed in battle). The optional drill
  (`ancient_drill`) is non-lethal and can't be fled.
- **The cardboard crew painter** (`src/art/characters/cardboardCrewPainter.js`): ordinary 32x48
  frames on the ordinary grid; flat poses (edge-on strip, a waddle on the nameplate, a spin with
  the plain back coming round, lying flat, a gust bending the card, lifted, panic, smoke, order);
  48x48 battle frames; portraits with 13 expressions each; Tilda's singed variant; the three
  dormant cut-outs as still props (`paintCutoutProp`), never animated.
- **A temporary Sharkstorm flare** (`src/systems/hazards/sharkstorm.js`): a state marked
  `temporary` can be lit over the saved state (`{ "sharkstorm": "fire_sharkstorm", "flare":
  true }`) and put out (`"end_flare"`), and burns itself out after `expiresAfterRooms` room
  changes (10). Conditions keep reading the saved state. The storm layer gains ember bursts and
  a faint orange sky flash (never with reduced effects).
- **Beard puffs** (`src/world/BeardPuffs.js`, `beard.puffs` in `data/story/beard.json`): while
  `beard_puffing` is set, every 20-36 seconds, a small puff and the nearest crewmate's short
  reaction; deterministic; the `beardPuff` command for scenes.
- **A pending release on Brogath's meter**: `pendingValue`, `countdownVar` and labels:
  COFFIN SOCK EVENT, PENDING, BREWING: n, IMMINENT.
- **CRT beats** for the documentary (a programme beat can wobble, roll or jitter the picture;
  reduced effects flicker only).
- **Presets with party members** (`"party": ["ledger", {"id": "ledger", "learn": [...]}]`).
- **Atmosphere states** FIRE_WAFT, THUNDERBOTTOM_RELEASE, FROG_GROG_FUMES; beard tags
  THUNDERBOTTOM_LEGENDARY, FROG_GROG_FUMES, COFFIN_SOCK_CATASTROPHE; Fart Zone releases
  `tilda_fire_waft` and `thunderbottom_grand`.

## Characters

- **Ledger Billiam** (party member and NPC): Brogath's treasurer; optimistic, financially
  delusional, loves a category; an enormous tricorn, a quill, a ledger. Support and economy.
- **No-Nonsense Tilda** (party member and NPC): first mate and navigator; strict, proud,
  stubborn, only somewhat more sensible, secretly sentimental; a broad green hat, eyebrows,
  arms folded. Command and defence. Singed at one corner from chapter 217 on.
- **Old Jeb Rattleplank, Wobbly Ned, Elsie Beanwright**: cut-outs in the crew quarters'
  gallery. Not reincarnated; future hooks only.
- **Lord Vaporious Thunderbottom the Seventh** (NPC, chapters 218-219): a ceremonial applicant
  at the Bureau, then history.
- Speakers: the documentary's narrator, the King's late-night voice, the hammerheads, the shark
  audience.
- **Garrick Grumblegut** stays the Grand Stenchmaster. **Brogath the Bashful** stays aboard and
  bashful (from this phase he may enter the hold and the Fart-Free Zone; never the treasure
  room). **Agnes the Second** survives everything, frazzled.

## Content rules kept

The names are the game's own: GARRICK GRUMBLEGUT, the Great Sharkstorm, Captain Stinkbeard,
the Queen Anne's Revenge. The Beard Joint and the Frog Grog are gags kept abstract and
off-screen: no preparation, amounts or method. Nothing graphic happens to Agnes; Thunderbottom's
threat is pompous, never violent. No unsupported resolutions: Greyhook is not reached, the
storm is not defeated, Brogath is not cured, the beard is not removed, nobody else comes back.
The tests scan the phase's data for all of this (`tests/phase17_story.test.js`).
